The idea graveyard

Every idea below was tested the same honest way: choose its setting on the six months before, trade it blind on the next quarter, six times in a row, and compare with changing nothing. Almost all of them did worse. Two passed and are live.

How to read this
  • Honest result is the profit in the six test windows minus the profit of the bot without the idea, as a percentage of the test account (3 lots of $100). Below zero means the idea did worse than doing nothing.
  • Windows better counts in how many of the six test windows (April 2025 to July 2026) the idea beat doing nothing. "—" means the idea had several versions and the source gives a range.
  • Every test pays fees and slippage on Binance 1-minute data. These are backtests: history, not a forecast. Not financial advice.
  • Shown: the 65 tests with one honest number. The research covered more (settings re-checks, money and lot sizing, risk caps); they are summarised on the Research page.
